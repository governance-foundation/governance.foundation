KnowledgeFund is not just a concept.
It can also be offered as a practical consulting engagement for organisations that want to build the internal capability layer AI actually depends on.

## Table of Contents


## Positioning

We do not sell an AI platform.

We help organisations build the internal knowledge, workflow, and governance structure that makes AI useful, repeatable, and scalable in the real business.

That means helping a company become more legible to itself before expecting AI to work coherently across the organisation.

## The core offer

KnowledgeFund Consulting helps an organisation:

- diagnose fragmentation and trapped knowledge
- identify the workflows and domains that matter most
- create the initial ontology and structural model
- connect work, decisions, systems, and context
- introduce AI assistance in grounded ways
- create contribution, governance, and reuse patterns
- form internal capability rather than only adopting tools

## Offer structure

### 1. Discovery and diagnostic

Goal: understand where the organisation's context is weak, fragmented, or hidden.

Typical activities:

- stakeholder interviews
- workflow discovery
- systems and context review
- knowledge bottleneck analysis
- AI readiness and capability review
- initial gap mapping

Deliverables:

- diagnostic summary
- opportunity map
- recommended pilot scope
- bootstrap roadmap

### 2. Pilot KnowledgeFund design

Goal: design the first bounded KnowledgeFund slice.

Typical activities:

- define pilot scope
- draft the first ontology
- map workflow, role, system, and artefact relationships
- define early governance patterns
- identify AI assistance opportunities
- define success measures

Deliverables:

- pilot KnowledgeFund model
- initial structural blueprint
- implementation backlog
- pilot success criteria

### 3. Pilot implementation

Goal: stand up the first live KnowledgeFund in one real domain.

Typical activities:

- capture workflow and tacit knowledge
- build the first connected knowledge layer
- connect key systems and context sources
- design contribution and update patterns
- introduce useful AI assistance
- support staff usage and adoption

Deliverables:

- functioning pilot KnowledgeFund
- initial ontology and knowledge structure
- embedded workflow usage pattern
- early measurement dashboard

### 4. Capability formation

Goal: make the organisation able to maintain and grow the KnowledgeFund itself.

Typical activities:

- internal coaching
- steward role definition
- quality and maintenance rules
- contribution rituals
- listening and gap-detection loops
- next-wave planning

Deliverables:

- internal operating model
- steward roles and responsibilities
- governance and maintenance patterns
- growth roadmap

### 5. Expansion program

Goal: extend the KnowledgeFund across more of the organisation.

Typical activities:

- add adjacent workflows and domains
- deepen the ontology
- connect more systems and artefacts
- standardise proven contribution patterns
- improve reporting and executive visibility
- strengthen cross-team reuse

Deliverables:

- scaled roadmap
- maturity model
- prioritised expansion waves

## Commercial packaging

This can be packaged in several ways:

- **Strategy sprint**: 2 to 4 weeks
- **Pilot bootstrap**: 6 to 12 weeks
- **Capability formation program**: 3 to 6 months
- **Ongoing advisory / embedded partner**

## Who it is for

This offer is most useful for organisations that:

- believe AI will matter across the business, not just in isolated tools
- know their knowledge and context are fragmented
- want to build internal capability, not just buy more software
- need a practical starting point rather than a giant abstract transformation program

## Why this framing matters

Most AI vendors sell infrastructure, tooling, or platforms.

But the real organisational challenge sits in the last mile:

- making work legible
- structuring context
- reducing hidden dependency
- improving reuse
- strengthening governance at the point of work
- turning AI presence into durable capability

That is the layer this offer is designed to help build.
