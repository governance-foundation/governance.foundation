## Why this section exists

These pages belong together.

They are not just miscellaneous notes. Together they define the current conceptual foundation for how Governance Foundation is thinking about:

- organisational ontology
- OntoUML and UFO interpretation
- type and identity discipline
- relators and richer relationship semantics
- events, time, and change
- enterprise architecture alignment
- runtime semantic structure
- glossary and working research backlog
