# Governance Foundation LinkedIn Narrative Schedule

A curated publishing path for LinkedIn that builds a coherent narrative from the earliest Governance Foundation thesis through the current KnowledgeFund, AI, and ontology work.

This is not just a date-order list.
It is a **reading and publishing sequence** designed to help an audience progressively understand:

1. why governance is a broader organisational problem than oversight/compliance
2. why frameworks and transformation programs keep underperforming
3. why the real bottleneck is fragmented organisational knowledge
4. why KnowledgeFund is the structural answer
5. why AI raises the urgency of this work
6. why the current model/ontology work matters now

---

## Recommended posting cadence

- **Preferred cadence:** 2 posts/articles per week
- **Alternative cadence:** 1 post/article per week for a slower, more reflective series
- **Suggested format:**
  - LinkedIn post = short framing + 1 key idea + link to full article
  - Full article = canonical long-form argument on site

At 2 per week, the primary sequence below runs for about 12 weeks.
